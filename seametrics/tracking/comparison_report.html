Tracking Metrics Comparison

▶ Metric glossary
MOTA↑Overall tracking quality — penalises misses, false positives and ID switches.
MOTP↑How accurately matched boxes overlap with ground truth (localisation precision).
IDF1↑F1 of correct ID assignments over the full sequence — rewards long, consistent tracks.
Num Switches↓Times a GT track is reassigned to a different predicted ID.
Num Misses↓GT detections with no matching prediction (false negatives).
Num False Positives↓Predictions with no matching GT object.
Mostly Tracked↑% of GT tracks covered by a single predicted ID for >80% of their lifespan.
Mostly Lost↓% of GT tracks covered by any predicted ID for <20% of their lifespan.
Precision / Recall↑Standard detection precision and recall across all frames.
HOTA↑Combined score of detection and association accuracy — the main HOTA summary metric.
DetA↑Detection accuracy component of HOTA — how well objects are detected.
AssA↑Association accuracy component of HOTA — how consistently IDs are maintained.
LocA↑Localisation accuracy component of HOTA — average IoU of matched detections.
